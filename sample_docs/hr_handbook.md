# Northstar Cloud | People Operations Handbook (DEMO DATA)
Northstar Cloud is a fictional SaaS company for illustrating document-grounded RAG.

## Leave and time off
Full-time employees receive 18 paid vacation days each calendar year. Employees should request planned leave at least five working days in advance. Medical leave can be reported on the day of absence.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Company devices must use full-disk encryption and the corporate VPN.

## Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning stipend of USD 600 for relevant certifications, courses, books, and conferences. Reimbursement requires manager approval and receipts.
