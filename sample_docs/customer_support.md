# Northstar Cloud | Customer Support Policy (DEMO DATA)
Northstar Cloud is a fictional SaaS company for illustrating document-grounded RAG.

## Service tiers
Standard support responds within two business days. Premium support responds within four business hours. Enterprise priority-one incidents have a 30-minute initial response target.

## Refunds
Annual subscriptions may request a refund within 14 calendar days of initial purchase. Monthly subscriptions are not refundable after a billing period begins. Approved refunds are processed in 5 to 10 business days.

## Escalation
For critical enterprise outages, page the on-call incident commander and create a priority-one ticket. Inform affected customers every 60 minutes while the incident remains open.
